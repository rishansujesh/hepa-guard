# AASLD MASLD Practice Guidance (Stub)

## Overview
MASLD evaluation should consider metabolic risk factors and liver-related biomarkers.

## Risk Stratification
Non-invasive fibrosis risk stratification is recommended to help prioritize follow-up.
Consider repeat labs and trend analysis when values are borderline.

### Fibrosis Assessment Tools
Use non-invasive tools when clinically indicated, and interpret within clinical context.

## Metabolic Risk Factors
Assess adiposity, glycemic status, and lipid markers as part of the risk profile.
Document alcohol intake and activity patterns.

## Lifestyle Counseling
Lifestyle counseling remains foundational and should be tailored to the individual.

## Follow-up and Monitoring
Reassess key labs over time and escalate if there is persistent abnormality.
